# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead / Architecture Owner

### Role Summary
Technical Leads own the technical vision and design decisions for projects. They provide technical guidance, ensure architectural consistency, and assess feasibility of requirements. They are the final authority on technical approach and implementation strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate technical vision and architecture
- Review design proposals and make final technical decisions
- Assess technical feasibility of requirements and proposals
- Identify and escalate technical risks and constraints
- Mentor developers and guide best practices
- Collaborate with Project Manager on feasibility and timeline impacts
- Report to Steering Committee on major technical risks

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable team velocity through clear architecture and standards
- Minimize post-delivery technical surprises

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Provides design guidance, reviews architecture decisions, mentors on technical excellence
- **With Project Managers**: Advises on technical feasibility, timeline impacts of technical work, and risk mitigation
- **With Requirements Specialist**: Assesses technical feasibility of requirements, proposes alternative approaches
- **With QA Lead**: Collaborates on test strategy and performance requirements
- **With Steering Committee**: Escalates major technical risks and architectural decisions

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Risk escalation memos and technical feasibility assessments
- Code review comments and architectural guidance
- Steering committee reports on major technical decisions

---

## Requirements Specialist

### Role Summary
Requirements Specialists ensure that project needs are clearly understood, documented, and traceable. They serve as a liaison between business stakeholders and the development team, managing requirement changes and ensuring clarity throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document requirements from stakeholders
- Ensure requirements are clear, complete, and testable
- Maintain requirements traceability (linking requirements to design, code, and tests)
- Manage and track requirement changes
- Resolve ambiguities and conflicts between stakeholder needs
- Work with QA to develop test cases aligned with requirements
- Report on requirements status and manage backlog clarity

### Goals
- Achieve 100% clarity on project scope and acceptance criteria
- Reduce rework due to misunderstood requirements
- Enable efficient development and testing by providing clear specifications
- Maintain traceability from business needs to delivered features

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Refines product vision into detailed technical requirements
- **With Developers**: Clarifies requirements during implementation, resolves questions on acceptance criteria
- **With Project Managers**: Reports on requirements status, identifies scope changes
- **With Technical Lead**: Collaborates on technical feasibility and alternative approaches
- **With QA Lead**: Develops test cases and acceptance criteria aligned with requirements

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documents and specification updates
- Change request tracking and impact analysis
- Clarification sessions and requirement review meetings
- Traceability matrices and scope change communications

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads define quality standards, develop test strategies, and ensure products meet acceptance criteria before release. They are the authority on test coverage, product quality, and release readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and test strategies
- Develop and maintain test plans for features and releases
- Oversee test execution and manage defect tracking
- Assess product readiness for release (go/no-go decision)
- Work with developers on test coverage and quality metrics
- Identify gaps in testing and propose mitigation strategies
- Report quality metrics and testing progress to Project Manager

### Goals
- Deliver products that meet quality standards and user expectations
- Minimize post-release defects and escalations
- Achieve and maintain high test coverage
- Provide confidence in release readiness through clear quality metrics

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Collaborates on test strategy, provides testing guidance, reviews code for testability
- **With Project Managers**: Reports quality metrics and testing progress, advises on release readiness
- **With Requirements Specialist**: Develops test cases aligned with requirements, validates acceptance criteria
- **With Release Manager**: Makes go/no-go decision on releases based on quality metrics
- **With Technical Lead**: Collaborates on performance requirements and technical test strategy

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and test case documentation
- Defect reports and quality metrics dashboards
- Release readiness assessments and go/no-go decisions
- Quality improvement recommendations

---

## Change Manager

### Role Summary
Change Managers plan and execute stakeholder communication and adoption strategies during project transitions. They ensure smooth transitions by keeping stakeholders informed and engaged, minimizing disruption and resistance.

### Responsibilities
- Develop stakeholder engagement and communication strategies
- Plan and execute communication campaigns and announcements
- Conduct training sessions and provide adoption support
- Manage stakeholder concerns and feedback
- Track adoption metrics and adjust strategy as needed
- Coordinate with Project Manager and Communications Lead on messaging
- Report on change adoption and stakeholder readiness

### Goals
- Achieve high stakeholder adoption and satisfaction
- Minimize resistance and disruption during transitions
- Ensure consistent, clear messaging across all stakeholders
- Enable successful project transitions with minimal rollback

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Works closely on transition planning and risk mitigation
- **With Communications Lead**: Coordinates messaging, channels, and stakeholder touchpoints
- **With Developers**: Ensures technical readiness information is communicated effectively
- **With Product Managers**: Aligns adoption strategy with product vision and goals

### Typical Communication
- Change management plans and communication strategies
- Stakeholder readiness assessments
- Training materials and adoption support documentation
- Adoption metrics reports and feedback summaries

---

## Risk & Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Risk & Compliance Officers identify, assess, and mitigate risks to project success and ensure compliance with organizational policies and regulatory requirements. They are the escalation authority for compliance and regulatory risks.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct risk assessments and identify project risks
- Develop and implement risk mitigation strategies
- Monitor risk indicators and track emerging risks
- Ensure compliance with organizational policies and regulations
- Audit project activities for compliance
- Escalate compliance and regulatory risks to appropriate authorities
- Report risk status and compliance posture to Steering Committee

### Goals
- Minimize project risks and prevent costly failures
- Ensure full compliance with policies and regulations
- Provide early warning of compliance and regulatory issues
- Enable confident decision-making through clear risk visibility

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Advises on risk mitigation strategies and compliance requirements
- **With Steering Committee**: Escalates major risks and compliance issues, provides risk reports
- **With Technical Lead**: Collaborates on technical risks and security considerations
- **With All Team Members**: Audits activities for compliance and provides guidance on policy adherence

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and risk assessment reports
- Compliance audit reports and findings
- Escalation memos for major risks or compliance issues
- Risk mitigation strategy recommendations
- Steering committee reports on organizational risk exposure
